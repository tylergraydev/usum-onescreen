Modifications: tools/gen_sharp_font.py rasterizes the English subset at 64 px,
quantizes coverage to 4 bits, crops each glyph to its ink and packs two
coverage samples per byte. The generated data is called SharpText. The original
TrueType file is retained unchanged to allow offline regeneration.
