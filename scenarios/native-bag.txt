# The native Bag (Screens/Bag.cpp, docs/BAG.md): opened from the native X
# menu with the panel off, every pocket browsed, a Potion used on a Pokémon
# hurt in a wild battle, items given to hold (also over a held item: Yes and
# No), a TM taught through the game's move-forget screen (and not learned),
# Exp. Share turned off and on, an item moved to the Free Space and back,
# and back out to the field. Every choice is made with buttons; the
# native Bag carries it out on the bottom screen. A screenshot is taken
# before every A that changes the game. Nothing is ever saved.
# Start in the Pokémon Center whatever ran before (native-move-learn leaves the grass).
restore pokecenter
include common/boot
# The automatic view would put the panel up in the Bag; the native Bag
# must hold it off.
send auto on

# --- Get Pichu hurt: wild battles until it is (switch Pichu in for a free hit, Run) ---
# Wild Pokémon differ: a Smeargle only knows Sketch, a Yungoos's Pursuit
# knocked Pichu out when it was switched out again, an Ekans's Wrap kept
# Pichu from running. So: one switch-in, Run until away, look at the party
# (hurt, but by at most 20 HP so a Potion fills it up).
mark
send stick 0 -150 120
expect-log ^scene: \(none\) 15
wait-scene FieldRo 30
sleep 6                   # the walk-out animation ignores the circle pad
repeat 8 until-scene Battle
  send stick 0 -150 60
  sleep 1.5
end
mark
repeat 4 until-log ^\s+4\s\S+\sPichu\(172\)\s"Pichu"\sL12\shp\s([7-9]|1[0-9]|2[0-6])/27
  repeat 40 until-scene Battle  # back and forth in the grass
    send stick -150 0 20
    sleep 0.5
    send stick 150 0 20
    sleep 0.5
  end
  sleep 12                  # battle intro, until the command menu shows
  shot battle
  # Pokémon -> slot 4 (Pichu) -> Switch: the wild Pokémon attacks Pichu.
  send move 35 75; tap 6
  sleep 2
  send move 247 112; tap 6
  sleep 2
  send move 182 194; tap 6
  sleep 16
  shot switched-in
  repeat 6 until-scene FieldRo
    send move 160 225; tap 6  # RUN
    sleep 20
    # A wrapped Pokémon (Ekans' Wrap) can't run and a refused Run takes no turn:
    # use a move so the trap wears off or the battle ends.
    if-scene Battle send move 285 120; tap 6  # Fight
    sleep 2
    if-scene Battle send move 235 45; tap 6   # first move
    sleep 16
  end
  sleep 3
  send party
end
shot field
