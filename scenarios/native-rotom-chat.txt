# Rotom's field prompts on the top screen (Screens/RotomPrompt.cpp,
# docs/ROTOM-FIELD.md), with the panel off throughout. About 5 minutes after
# Continue, Rotom wants to talk: the badge shows and Up (the D-pad) taps
# Rotom's eye on the hidden bottom screen. Rotom then starts a chat or the
# Roto Loto (its choice). A Roto Loto is played through with the prompt
# (rotom next = A) and Rotom asked again when it is ready, until a chat
# comes. The chat's question and answers show on the top screen; Down moves
# to the second answer and A taps it. The native map, opened while Rotom
# asks, steps aside and comes back after. Screenshots before every A.
# Start in the Pokémon Center whatever ran before (native-move-learn leaves the grass).
restore pokecenter
include common/boot
expect-log ^rotom: applet [0-9A-F]+, FieldMenu at [0-9A-F]+, chat table ok 30

# --- Rotom wants to talk (the badge), or starts a chat by itself ---
# Both came within about 5 minutes of Continue in the runs; allow 12.
mark
repeat 72 until-log ^rotom:\s(ready\s\(talk\skey\sUp\)|choice\s)
  sleep 10
end
expect-log ^rotom: drawing 5
expect-mode off
sleep 1
shot first

# --- Up: on the badge it starts the chat (a tap on Rotom's eye); on a
# question that is already up it is the first answer, where the cursor is ---
send press up
sleep 2

# --- A chat, or the Roto Loto first: play that through and ask again ---
# `rotom next` is A on a Roto Loto step and `rotom talk` is Up on the
# badge; each is refused (logged) when there's nothing for it.
repeat 120 until-log ^rotom:\schoice\s
  sleep 3
  send rotom next
  send rotom talk
end
# Each Roto Loto line, if one came, was read from the game's text.
reject-log ^rotom: roto loto (intro|result|power) ""$
reject-log ^rotom: .* not available
expect-scene FieldRo
expect-mode off
sleep 2
shot choice

# --- The map steps aside while Rotom asks, and opens again after ---
mark
send press zr
expect-log ^map: closed \(Rotom\) 5
expect-mode off

# --- Down: the second answer; A taps it on the bottom screen ---
mark
send press down
expect-log ^rotom: cursor 2 5
sleep 1
shot pre-answer
send press a
expect-log ^rotom: answer 2 "[^"]+" 5
expect-log ^action: rotom-answer-2 ok 5
expect-log ^rotom: answered 2 10
expect-log ^rotom: reply "[^"]+" 5
reject-log ^rotom: answer 2 not taken
reject-log ^rotom: the chat ended without the native prompt
sleep 1
shot reply
expect-log ^rotom: none 15
expect-log ^map: open \(Rotom\) 10
expect-log ^map: drawing 10
expect-scene FieldRo
expect-mode off
sleep 1
shot after
send press zr
expect-log ^map: closed \(ZR\) 5

check-save
stop
